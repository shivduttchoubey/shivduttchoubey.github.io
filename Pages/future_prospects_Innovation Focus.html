Frontier Innovation Focus

Translating theoretical cryptography and systems research into real-world impact and protected IP.

1. Healthcare Data Security & LiveMedQR

Medical data is among the most sensitive personal information in existence. My prototype LiveMedQR directly tackles clerical and transcription vulnerabilities in hospitals by attaching a dedicated encrypted Wi-Fi unit to clinical monitoring machines.

Telemetry data is encrypted at the source and transmitted directly to the patient's personal device, building accurate Electronic Healthcare Records (EHR) while preserving end-to-end data privacy.

  • Eliminating manual transcription error rates committed by clinical staff.
  • Protecting medical record integrity using tamper-evident cryptographic checksums.
  • Aligning medical IoT telemetry with healthcare compliance standards and India's DPDP Act.

3. Privacy by Design & AI Regulatory Engineering

As AI models and automated systems ingest massive amounts of data, engineering "Privacy by Design" is non-negotiable. I integrate regulatory compliance (DPDP Act, GDPR, EU AI Act) directly into the code and architecture.

  • Embedding data minimization and consent verification into backend API pipelines.
  • Auditing machine learning workflows against the EU AI Act risk tiers and ISO/IEC 42001 AI management baselines.
  • Implementing privacy-preserving machine learning via Secure Federated Learning and differential privacy.
