Research & Industry Collaborations

Fostering interdisciplinary partnerships across academia, defense, industry, and open standards.

1. Academic & Frontier Research Labs

My collaborative experience stems from working closely with premier institutions, including the National Forensic Sciences University (NFSU), Gandhinagar, the Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur (IIT-KGP), and participating in workshops hosted by C-DAC Pune and IIT Delhi.

I am actively interested in joint research initiatives focusing on Post-Quantum Cryptography benchmark standards, quantum network repeaters, and distributed digital forensics architectures.

  • Co-authoring peer-reviewed research papers and technical pre-prints.
  • Joint simulation modeling on the Qniverse quantum simulation platform.
  • Sharing novel cryptographic dataset benchmarks and reproducible test harnesses.

2. Industry, Defense & Telecommunications Sectors

As critical infrastructure faces imminent post-quantum migration deadlines, I welcome partnerships with telecommunications operators, cloud infrastructure providers, and healthcare networks.

  • Validating Harvest-Now-Decrypt-Later (HNDL) exposure across enterprise cloud object storage.
  • Piloting lightweight Netflow IDPS deployments for resource-constrained SMB networks.
  • Telemetry privacy trials for connected medical devices (LiveMedQR).

3. Open-Source Consortia & Community Education

I firmly believe that robust security cannot remain confined to closed proprietary systems. I actively collaborate with open-source security projects and civic advocacy initiatives like WeSeSo (We Secure Society).

  • Open-sourcing defensive security tools and Python CLI utilities.
  • Delivering guest lectures, workshops, and cyber awareness drives for academic and civic communities.
  • Mentoring emerging cybersecurity students in digital forensics and offensive-defensive fundamentals.
