About Me

Hello! I'm Shiv Dutt Choubey, a Cybersecurity graduate candidate pursuing an M.Sc. in Cyber Security at the premier National Forensic Sciences University (NFSU), Gandhinagar (Current SPI: 9.05), and a former NPTEL Pre-Doctoral Fellow at the Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur (IIT-KGP).

My core research and engineering focus lies at the intersection of Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC), Quantum Internet Architecture, and AI-driven Network Intrusion Detection & Prevention Systems (IDPS). I investigate defensive measures against emerging Harvest-Now-Decrypt-Later (HNDL) quantum threats, developing semantic post-decryption security layers (ERIV) and risk-scoring engines for cloud storage (QuantumPosture).

With an academic foundation in Computer Science (B.Sc., 80%), I combine mathematical cryptographic foundations with rigorous systems programming in Modern C++, Python, and Linux internals. I have practical experience designing conversational AI data pipelines at Doions Pvt. Ltd. and researching software patent landscapes at RGSOIPL, IIT Kharagpur.

Beyond technical systems, I am deeply knowledgeable in global regulatory and data privacy frameworks, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, the GDPR, and the EU AI Act. I implement enterprise security controls aligned with NIST CSF, ISO/IEC 27001, and ISO/IEC 42001, dedicated to engineering resilient, tamper-proof systems for critical infrastructure.
