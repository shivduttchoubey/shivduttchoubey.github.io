Strategic Research Goals

Advancing Post-Quantum Cryptography, Quantum Networks, and Resilient Cyber Defense.

1. Mitigating Harvest-Now-Decrypt-Later (HNDL) Threats

In the contemporary threat landscape, sophisticated adversaries actively intercept and stockpile encrypted communications today, intending to decrypt them once cryptanalytically relevant quantum computers (CRQCs) materialize. My primary research goal is designing robust architectural defenses that protect data longevity.

Building upon my work with QuantumPosture and the ERIV semantic protection layer, my research formalizes post-decryption security barriers, automated cloud object storage risk scoring, and post-quantum cryptographic migration blueprints.

  • Formalizing quantitative exposure scoring based on data retention windows and encryption algorithm vulnerability.
  • Implementing lattice-based Key Encapsulation Mechanisms (ML-KEM) and digital signature schemes (ML-DSA).
  • Developing semantic security barriers that render intercepted ciphertexts unusable even after post-quantum decryption.

2. Quantum Internet Architecture & PQC Networking

As a former NPTEL Pre-Doctoral Research Fellow at IIT Kharagpur under the sponsored project "Design and Development of Future Quantum Internet Architecture and Post-Quantum Modern Networking", I investigate the protocols required for next-generation quantum networks.

This involves modeling quantum entanglement routing, quantum repeater interfaces, and quantum key distribution (QKD) simulation using platforms such as Qniverse.

  • Designing quantum communication protocols that resist decoherence and eavesdropping in transit.
  • Simulating Quantum Gates, Grover's Algorithm, and Quantum Error Correction (QEC) for secure multi-party communication.
  • Investigating hybrid classical-quantum network interfaces for seamless backward compatibility.

3. Lightweight AI-Driven IDPS for Critical & SMB Infrastructure

Traditional enterprise IDPS tools impose massive computational overhead, making them inaccessible for small-to-medium businesses and low-power IoT/edge devices. My research focuses on resource-optimized anomaly detection leveraging Netflow telemetry and secure machine learning.

  • Engineering high-precision anomaly detection with minimal false-positive rates on constrained hardware.
  • Integrating MITRE ATT&CK tactics directly into automated packet filtering pipelines.
  • Applying Secure Federated Learning so multi-tenant networks can collaboratively train intrusion models without leaking private payload data.

4. Abstract Syntax Tree (AST) & Compiler Security

Investigating pre-compilation source code verification using Abstract Syntax Tree (AST) graph analysis combined with Natural Language Processing embeddings to intercept unauthorized source code tampering, dependency poisoning, and malicious logic bombs before binary generation.

  • Automating semantic code drift detection across continuous integration and deployment pipelines.
  • Detecting stealthy backdoors inserted into open-source dependencies prior to software compilation.
